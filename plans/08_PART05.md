## P05-S05 — UrbanSim

**File:** `drivex/scenes/part05/p05_s05_urbansim.py`
**Class:** `P05S05UrbanSim`
**Background:** WHITE
**Duration:** ~75s

### What
- Bottleneck of traditional sim: CPU↔GPU ping-pong → 180 GPU days.
- UrbanSim: GPU-native (Omniverse). All on GPU.
- Async scene sampling.
- Result: 2620 FPS, 256 envs, 11.2 GB VRAM, 180 GPU days → 3 hours.
- Real-world validation: PPO-UrbanVerse beats S2E, CityWalker, NoMaD on COCO + Unitree Go2.

### Critical fixes
1. **3DGS and Mesh rectangles**: when they appear, place them with vertical separation — don't overlap.
2. **Don't pull them together** later — if you do, the "appearance" label overlaps the center. Either keep them apart OR move the label up.
3. **Sim-to-real arrow shrinking** is awkward because the sim and real boxes don't move. Either:
   - Keep arrow same size, just have sim and real boxes get CLOSER to each other (they DO move), OR
   - Visualize the gap with a `Brace` between fixed boxes that visually narrows.
4. **Text size** — bump it up.

   Before:  [Sim] ←─────large gap─────→ [Real]    ← brace/double arrow
